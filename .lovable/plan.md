

# Brutal Elegance — Artisan Silk Homepage

A cinematic, gallery-like homepage for a luxury handwoven silk brand from Odisha, India. The design language is "A24 film meets high-end art gallery."

## Global Setup
- Import Google Fonts: Cormorant Garamond, Montserrat (300), Noto Serif Devanagari
- Set base background to `#FDFCF0`, dark sections to `#121212`, accent `#8B0000`
- Build a transparent navbar that fades in with blur on scroll-up: **The Provenance | The Artifacts | Speak to a Stylist**

## Section 1: The Hero — "The Arrival"
- Full-screen section with a placeholder cinematic background (dark, moody textile close-up aesthetic using CSS gradients and subtle animation since we can't embed real video)
- A single "thwack-clack" loom sound effect triggered once on page load
- Large centered Hindi text: *मेरे हाथों की लकीरें, इस रेशम में बुन गई हैं।*
- Smaller English subtitle below
- Bottom-left micro-copy: *"Woven in the dust of Odisha. Draped in the boardrooms of the world."*
- Bottom-right: *"Enter the Loom"* underline link that scrolls down
- Slow fade-and-rise entrance animations (800ms+)

## Section 2: The Philosophy — "The Contrast"
- Asymmetric layout: large negative space left, content block right
- Placeholder for dramatic B&W weaver portrait
- Hindi headline + English body copy manifesto about luxury born from grit
- "Read Our Provenance" button

## Section 3: The Artifacts — "The Collection"
- Staggered two-column gallery grid with varying image heights
- 3-4 sample product cards with placeholder images alternating between draped saree and raw materials
- Each card: title, specs (hours, material), price as raw number (e.g., "24,000")
- Hover interaction: slow image zoom + text morphs to *"Claim this Heirloom"*

## Section 4: The Voice — "The Raw Truth"
- Full-width `#121212` charcoal block breaking the page
- Large centered Hindi headline + English poem/manifesto in `#FDFCF0`
- Right-aligned signature: *— Sanjukta, Master Weaver, Nuapatna Village*
- Wide margins forcing the eye to center

## Section 5: The Footer — "The Exit"
- Minimal footer on `#FDFCF0` with vast negative space
- Newsletter: *"Leave your email to receive private viewings..."* with email input + "Join the Inner Circle" button
- Bottom links: The Craft | Private Concierge | Care for your Heirloom | Shipping & Ethics

## Motion & Interaction Throughout
- Scroll-triggered fade-and-rise animations (800ms+) for all text sections
- Slow subtle scale on image hover
- No bouncy or snappy transitions — everything cinematic and deliberate
